FROGGLE 0 pts

How to play

  • Get the frog from the FROM bank to the TO bank by typing a word that links them.
  • A lily pad grows where your word sits between the two. The more it leans toward the far bank, the further out it grows.
  • The frog can only jump half the river. Too close to the far bank and it falls in: you lose a life, and you have 3.
  • A word right in the middle crosses in one hop.

Scoring

  • Fewer hops score more: 10 / 6 / 4 / 2 base points.
  • Stronger links earn up to +50%: gold, silver, bronze.
  • Some pads have a fly 🪰. Land there and the frog eats it for bonus points.
  • +2 for every life left at the end.
A new puzzle every day at midnight.
